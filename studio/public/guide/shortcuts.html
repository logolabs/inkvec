Keyboard and mouse

On a Mac, read ⌘ for Ctrl. Every drag in the viewer has a key and a single click or tap that does the same, and every drag in the palette has a button (tick and Merge, or a chip's ×).

Anywhere

Keys Does
Ctrl+O Open an image
Ctrl+V Open the image on the clipboard (a screenshot, or a picture copied with Copy image), outside a text field
Ctrl+E Open the export sheet (once there is a drawing)
Ctrl+, Settings
F1 This guide, at the page about what is on screen
Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+9, Ctrl+0 Choose a preset: Logo, Icon, Fine detail, Fewer paths, Photo or scan, One-colour logo, Black no background, Two-tone scan, Line art, Editable
Esc Close, in this order: a dialog or the export sheet; Settings, About, Showcase or the guide; the Custom wizard (keeping what you chose); the choice card (keeping Auto). With none of those open, cancel the trace in progress.

The preset shortcuts do nothing while you are typing in a field.

In a dialog

The export sheet, Settings, About, Showcase, the guide and every dialog over the window behave the same way:

Keys Does
(opening it) The keyboard moves into it
Tab, Shift+Tab Move between its controls; past the last one it goes back to the first, never to the window behind
Esc Close it; the keyboard goes back to the button that opened it

The window behind is out of reach while one is open, for the keyboard and for a screen reader.

In the Vectorize viewer

Keys or gesture Does
Hold Space Show the source instead of the SVG (A/B), until released
0, or double-click a pane Fit the drawing to the window
+ or = Zoom in
- Zoom out
Scroll wheel, or pinch Zoom about the pointer
Drag Pan
The arrow buttons beside the zoom stops Pan a quarter of the view (shown once you have zoomed or panned)
Tab to the viewer, then the arrow keys Pan a tenth of the view; with Shift, half
Drag the wipe divider, or tap where it should go Move it (in Wipe)
Tab to the wipe divider, then ← → Move it 2% of the width; with Shift, or Page Up and Page Down, 10%; Home and End go to the ends

Space also flicks between Before and After in the Minify SVG tab, whose wipe divider works the same way.

In the rail

Keys Does
Tab Move between controls
Arrow keys, on a slider Move it one step; each step traces
Enter, in a number field Apply the value
Space, on a palette tick box Tick or untick the ink
Focus a control's name Show what it does
Focus an ink or a colour group Single it out in the drawing
Enter, in the save-preset or colour field Save or use it
Tab to the engine log's lines Scroll them with the arrow keys
